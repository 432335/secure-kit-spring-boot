# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but discuss technical disagreements directly.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Do not publish private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from the project spaces.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
